Kenya Wildlife service

HISTORY

The Kenya Wildlife Service (KWS) is a state corporation established in 1989 under the Ministry of Tourism and Wildlife. It was formed to conserve and manage Kenya’s national parks, wildlife sanctuaries, and protected areas. This came as a direct intervention during a severe wildlife crisis, particularly the rapid decline of elephants and rhinos due to heavy poaching. Prior to its creation, wildlife management was handled by the precursor Wildlife Conservation and Management Department, which faced severe resource and anti-poaching challenges

MISSION

The mission of the Kenya Wildlife Service is to conserve and manage Kenya’s wildlife and their habitats for future generations, while promoting sustainable use and enjoyment of these resources.

VISION

The vision of the Kenya Wildlife Service is to be a world-class wildlife conservation organization, recognized for its excellence in wildlife management, research, and community engagement.
